# Project Desicions
## Cohort Selection
After previewing the datasets with gdc_exploration.ipynb, TCGA-KIRC (kidney renal clear cell carcinoma) was determined to be the best dataset to learn with. 972 tumor to 480 normal samples made it the best ratio (2:1) of all the datasets, and my background in a urology lab makes this intriguing research. Other datasets also have ample samples and could be good to practice class imbalance in future projects.
